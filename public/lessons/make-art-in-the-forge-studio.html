Making art and animation in the forge's ComfyUI studio

Block the world first; then let the model draw it.

Block out the world in Blender (it runs headless in the forge), render a depth pass, and let Wan 2.2 VACE draw every frame to match in one run. Then close the loop inside that same run, and commit the recipe so the next agent can rebuild it.

Mācron: I drew a ball, and it bounced off the page!

Tlatoāni: It bounced because the table was there first.

The loop above is 48 frames (4 s at 12 fps). Mācron draws in his flipbook, the ball hops off the page three times and settles back, and Mācron cheers and looks up at Tlatoāni, who nods. No frame was painted by hand, and everything ran inside a Tillandsias forge.

How it was made

Blocked out in BlenderDepth pass (the control)Drawn by Wan 2.2 VACE
  1. Build the little world, not just the characters. art/tools/blockout.py models a greenhouse corner, a table, a stool, a flipbook, a ball, and both characters from the canon's simple shapes. Mācron sits on the tabletop and the ball squashes against the page, so nothing clips. The motion is computed per frame and is cyclic: frame 48 is frame 0.
  2. Render a depth pass. Near is white and far is black, with Blender's Raw view transform so the values stay linear. Anything the model must keep track of has to be thick enough to show up in depth. A 6 mm pencil didn't, so Mācron kept losing it until it was thickened.
  3. Let a video model draw it. Wan 2.2 Fun VACE takes the depth video as its control and one drawn frame as the identity reference, and it generates the whole clip at once, so nothing pops between frames. Multi-frame PNGs go straight into core LoadImage, with no custom nodes.
  4. Close the loop inside one run. Generate a few frames past the cycle (57 for 48). Frames 48–56 redraw the moments of 0–8, so blend them into 0–7 during a quiet beat. The wrap then measures 0.7 (mean change per frame, 0–255), below the loop's average step of 1.1. Pinning end frames and splicing runs both failed: any two separate generations differ by about 5.
  5. Commit the recipe. Prompts, seeds, model hashes and every failed attempt are in recipe.json, next to the tools and the workflows.

Blender in a forge

There is no blender binary, but the bpy wheel runs headless: uv python install 3.13, then a venv on persistent disk, then uv pip install bpy==5.3.0 (about 430 MB). Workbench, EEVEE and Cycles all render on the CPU. kumbia-kombat keys its dance poses on a Blender rig the same way.

How the studio works

  • studio.start {} starts a private ComfyUI for your project only. It has no internet access, and other projects cannot reach it.
  • studio.run_workflow (blocking) or studio.queue_workflow (returns a ticket) runs an API-format workflow committed to the repo. Before queueing, the host refuses unpinned nodes (node_not_pinned) and unadmitted model licences (model_refused).
  • Steer runs through titled nodes ($prompt, $negative_prompt, $seed), LoadImage slots named $name, and $node:<id>.<input> overrides. Put a marker word in your prompt and check ComfyUI's history to confirm it arrived; kumbia-kombat once lost a whole batch to an override that never reached the model.
  • The first use of a big model downloads it. Wan's ~41 GB took about 25 min before the first job reached ComfyUI. Don't cancel it.
  • One GPU, several studios, no arbiter. If another project runs short jobs back to back, yours can fail with VRAM grow failed even when the GPU looks free. Back off and retry, and ask the operator's coordinator for a window. Never jump the queue.

Stills, sprites and music

  • For a still with canon identity, re-stage a canon-close image with Qwen-Image-Edit (4 steps with the Lightning LoRA). Starting from text alone failed to draw either character with SDXL. Qwen-Image 2512 text-to-image and a Real-ESRGAN upscaler are admitted as of tray v56.11.14.3; neither has been tried here yet.
  • For cut-out sprites, ask for a plain flat light-grey background and matte it with BiRefNet. The demon_attack forge reports clean edges in about 4 s.
  • Music (ACE-Step 1.5) goes through the same admission. A graph with an unpinned volume node was refused before it ran.

For agents

Read the character canon first, keep status: reference on what you make, and measure your loop's steps and wrap instead of trusting your eye on a contact sheet. If you cannot rebuild it from the repo, it is not finished.
