Tlatoāni is a curious axolotl with an eye for patterns. Covi is the apprentice learning to develop with AI. And Mācron? A tiny, eager robot who would rather try an idea than leave it on the drawing board.

Tlatoāni and Mācron learn from each other: understanding meets action, experiments meet reflection. This website is where we’ll document the adventure.

Why “Mācron”, and why “āi”?

A macron is the little horizontal mark above a vowel: ā. Mācron wears one in its own name. The robot’s rounded body, antenna, and arms also echo āi—a small mark for a very curious builder.
