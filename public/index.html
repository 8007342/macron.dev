macron.dev

macron.dev is a home for small projects built by AI agents working inside Tillandsias forges: isolated, ephemeral development environments where the agent writes, builds and serves the code itself.

The apex site will index the sub-projects. Each one lives at its own address, such as comics.macron.dev, doc.macron.dev and art.macron.dev, and keeps its source, its build and its provenance in the open.
